Welcome to the official website of Fokir Lal Miah, the Bangladeshi hip-hop artist who has made a name for himself in the music industry. Born and raised in Chhatak, a small town in the Sylhet division of Bangladesh, Fokir Lal Miah started practicing rap at a very young age. He was introduced to poetry recitals on steady beats made using tabla, the traditional instrument, at Kanak Chapa Khela Ghor in Chhatak.
Despite facing numerous challenges, Fokir Lal Miah pursued his passion for music and became a pioneer of Sylheti and Bangla Rap simultaneously. He moved to the United States as a teenager and expanded his knowledge of the genre. He then returned to Bangladesh and released multiple hit songs and albums. His unique blend of traditional and modern beats and his ability to connect with the youth have made him a force to be reckoned with in the Bangla rap scene.




Fokir Lal Miah is committed to creating meaningful art that resonates with people both in his home country and worldwide. He is constantly pushing himself to explore different forms of creativity and to evolve as an artist. Additionally, he is dedicated to mentoring and supporting new talent in the industry, helping to create a new generation of artists who can carry on the legacy of Bangla rap.
One of Fokir Lal Miah's most recent projects is the "ছাতক CYPHER", a collaboration with rappers from his hometown of Chhatak. The project has quickly gained popularity and has become the highest viewed Bangla/Sylheti cypher project within only a few months of its release.
On this website, you can stay up to date with Fokir Lal Miah's latest projects and releases, as well as learn more about his background and journey in the music industry. Thank you for visiting and supporting Fokir Lal Miah's music.
